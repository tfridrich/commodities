  • Exchange rates are the euro foreign exchange reference rates of the European Central Bank, published for information on every TARGET working day at about 16:00 CET. USD/CNY and GBP/USD are cross rates calculated from them, so they can differ slightly from market quotes at the same moment.
  • Inflation for the euro area, Germany and Austria is the harmonised index of consumer prices (HICP), which the ECB targets at 2%; the national consumer price index of Germany (VPI) can differ by a few tenths. The latest month is often a flash estimate that is revised once. US inflation is calculated here from the CPI for all urban consumers (CPI-U, not seasonally adjusted); UK inflation is the ONS consumer prices index (CPI). Chinese inflation is not shown because there is no free source for it that is kept up to date and can be read automatically.
  • Construction prices and costs are the German indices for new residential buildings (Destatis, published by Eurostat). Germany's indices for office and industrial buildings move closely with them but are published by Destatis only.
  • The labour cost index covers wages and salaries plus employers' social contributions and payroll taxes, minus subsidies, per hour actually worked. Where Eurostat publishes a seasonally and calendar adjusted series it is shown; otherwise the unadjusted index has a regular pattern within the year (one-off payments, holidays).
  • All indices are rebased to their 2021 average = 100 so different sources can be compared; the "1 year" change is against the same quarter a year earlier.
